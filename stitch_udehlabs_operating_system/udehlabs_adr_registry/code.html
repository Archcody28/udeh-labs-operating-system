ADR-002

Implementation of Multi-Tenant Vector Store for Contextual Intelligence

Author Dr. A. Turing
Date 2023-10-27
Status PROPOSED

info Context

As we expand our AI architecture capabilities, the need to store and retrieve contextual embeddings for multiple client organizations concurrently has grown. Currently, we utilize isolated Postgres instances with pgvector, which is proving difficult to scale horizontally and manage efficiently in a multi-tenant SaaS environment.

warning Problem

Cost of infrastructure is scaling linearly with new tenant onboarding. Isolation mechanisms are manual and prone to misconfiguration. Query latency spikes during high-load periods due to lack of dedicated indexing resources for vector spaces.

check_circle Decision

We will migrate from isolated pgvector instances to a centralized, managed Pinecone Enterprise cluster, utilizing namespaces for strict logical tenant isolation and leveraging their hybrid search (sparse/dense) capabilities.

compare_arrows Alternatives & Tradeoffs

1. Milvus (Self-Hosted)

Rejected due to high operational overhead and complexity in managing distributed consensus and storage nodes.

2. Qdrant (Cloud)

Strong contender, but Pinecone's serverless offering provides better elasticity for our specific bursty workload patterns.

psychology_alt Consequences

  • Significant reduction in operational management overhead.
  • Vendor lock-in risk increases; mitigation requires abstraction layer (LangChain/LlamaIndex integration).
  • Predictable cost scaling model based on read/write units rather than raw compute provisioning.
