# Phase A.58: Enterprise Data Intelligence, Analytics & Decision Intelligence Architecture

## Overview
Phase A.58 establishes the **Intelligence & Decision Layer**, transforming UdehLabs from an operational platform into a self-aware, data-driven ecosystem. It unifies telemetry from every previous phase—Engineering, AI, Billing, and Security—into a governed analytics fabric that provides high-fidelity observability, predictive forecasting, and actionable recommendations.

## Core Components

### 1. Data Intelligence Fabric (`IDataIntelligenceOrchestrator`)
- **Event Normalization**: Uses existing event infrastructure (A.0-A.57) to normalize operational events into analytics-ready records with consistent schemas.
- **Governed Metric Definitions**: Centralizes the logic for metrics (e.g., "Deployment Success Rate," "AI Token Efficiency") in a machine-readable registry, preventing formula drift across the UI.
- **Query Authorization**: Enforces strict tenant isolation and RBAC at the query level, ensuring analytics never become a vector for cross-tenant data exfiltration.

### 2. Observability & SLI/SLO Engine (`ISLOService`)
- **Service Health Mapping**: Aggregates signals from logs, metrics, and traces to define service states: `HEALTHY`, `DEGRADED`, or `UNHEALTHY`.
- **Error Budget Management**: Calculates consumed vs. remaining error budgets based on configurable Service Level Objectives (SLOs).
- **Anomaly Detection**: Implements controlled detection of statistical outliers in traffic, latency, and cost, providing evidence-based explanations rather than raw alerts.

### 3. Decision Intelligence & Forecasting (`IDecisionSupportService`)
- **Predictive Analytics**: Provides forecasts for resource usage, AI consumption, and revenue trends based on historical data patterns.
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Generates governed suggestions (e.g., "Review budget for rapidly increasing AI usage") anchored in real platform evidence.
- **Recommendation Lifecycle**: Manages the flow from `SUGGESTED` -> `REVIEWED` -> `ACCEPTED`, ensuring no autonomous action bypasses human authorization.

### 4. Natural Language Analytics (`IAIAnalyticsAgent`)
- **Grounded NL-to-Query**: Translates natural language questions into approved, bounded analytics queries, avoiding unrestricted SQL execution.
- **Causal Analysis Framework**: Correlates deployments, commits, and incidents to assist in root-cause analysis (RCA) without assuming correlation equals causation.

## Data Models
- **MetricDefinition**: The registry of truth for how metrics are calculated.
- **MetricMeasurement / TimeSeries**: Aggregated, period-bound data points for performance.
- **AnalyticsDashboard / Widget**: Configurable structural units for data visualization.
- **Anomaly / Forecast**: Structured records of statistical outliers and predictions.
- **SLODefinition / ErrorBudget**: The governance records for reliability targets.
- **DataQualityCheck**: Monitors the integrity and freshness of the analytics pipeline.

## Security & Privacy
- **Zero-Trust Analytics**: Every query validates actor scope and resource classification (A.52).
- **Non-Surveillance Policy**: Explicitly avoids individual employee surveillance metrics, focusing on team and organizational outcomes.
- **Secret Redaction**: Telemetry and analytics pipelines redact PII and credentials before storage or AI analysis.

## Vertical Integration
- **A.57 Integration**: Uses the Knowledge Fabric to ground analytics explanations in historical context.
- **A.56 Integration**: Powers AI Agents with analytics tools for autonomous decision support.
- **A.53/A.54 Integration**: Feeds financial and customer health analytics into executive mission control.
