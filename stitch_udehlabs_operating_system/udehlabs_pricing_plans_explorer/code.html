Frequently Asked Billing Questions

info How is token usage calculated?

Tokens are metered per API call based on the complexity of the financial model being executed. Unused tokens roll over for one billing cycle.

receipt_long What is the refund policy?

We offer prorated refunds for seat cancellations within the first 14 days of the billing cycle. Annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee.

group_add How does seat scaling work?

You can add seats at any time. You will be billed a prorated amount for the new seats on your next invoice. Removing seats applies as a credit.
