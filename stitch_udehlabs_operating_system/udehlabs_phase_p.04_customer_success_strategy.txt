# UdehLabs | Phase P.04 Customer Success & Retention Strategy

## 1. Customer Health Model (`ICustomerHealthEngine`)
We define "Health" not as logins, but as **Value Extraction**.
- **Signals**: 
    - `Activation`: Completed onboarding and first AI task (Weight: 30%)
    - `Engineering Velocity`: Weekly agent executions and PR merges (Weight: 40%)
    - `Team Depth`: Number of active squad members (Weight: 20%)
    - `Financial Health`: Current credit balance vs. burn rate (Weight: 10%)
- **States**:
    - **HEALTHY**: Consistent value extraction; credits active.
    - **NEEDS ATTENTION**: Drop in agent executions (>30% WoW).
    - **AT RISK**: Subscription past due or zero activity for 14 days.
    - **DORMANT**: No activity for 30+ days; workspace archived.

## 2. Support Operations & Service Desk
- **Unified Entry Point**: Integrated Support Center within the Client Portal.
- **Ticket Lifecycle**: `OPEN` -> `IN_PROGRESS` -> `WAITING_FOR_CLIENT` -> `RESOLVED`.
- **Traceability**: Support tickets link directly to `EngineeringTasks` or `BilingRecords`.
- **AI Support Assistant**: Grounded in the Phase A.57 Knowledge Fabric to provide accurate technical answers without human intervention for Tier 1 issues.

## 3. Feedback & Roadmap Loop
- **Classification**: Bugs, Features, Performance, Security.
- **Aggregation**: Repeated issues trigger "Product Insight" alerts for the engineering team.
- **Closing the Loop**: Automatic notifications to clients when a reported bug is fixed in a production release.

## 4. Retention & Expansion Signals
- **Expansion**: Reaching 80% of agent limits or consistent 20% WoW growth in compute usage.
- **Retention**: Proactive "Efficiency Reports" generated by AI to show the cost-benefit of autonomous squads.
